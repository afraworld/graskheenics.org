The Tafheena Framework

Conceptual Model

Existing models describe either optimized performance (flow, NDM) or system collapse (freeze, dissociation). The transitional boundary between these states — where deliberative control yields while action coherence persists — lacks a unified description.

Tafheena proposes a three-layer control handover architecture: a deliberative control layer (H1) governing optimization; a procedural continuity layer (H2) enabling rapid embodied execution; and an arbitration layer (H3) that monitors for perceived irreversibility and redistributes control authority.

Perceived irreversibility — not danger alone — functions as the weighting signal. When the system interprets a situation as approaching a point of no return, H3 shifts dominance to H2, preserving trajectory coherence before explicit reasoning can respond.

Yuliana Grasman is a framework developer, author, and researcher specializing in internal navigation, decision architecture, and post-pressure adaptation. She is the originator of Graskheenics and the Survival Architecture framework, which explore how human agents stabilize decision-making and maintain internal continuity under conditions of irreversible consequence and high structural pressure.

// Lineage & Foundational Discipline

Grasman's research is built upon a multi-decade lineage of physiological and psychological discipline. Her methodology is rooted in a 1990 clinical "Self-Regulation" curriculum established by her father, a physical therapy specialist. This inherited discipline provided a biological default for internal stabilization, which Grasman evolved through thirty years of application in extreme operational and cultural environments.

// Lived Extraction & Phenomenological Data

Her primary research output, the Tafheena framework, is a lived extraction derived from over a decade of participant observation in Gulf motorsports (2012–2023). Documented in regional media — Khaleej Times (2017), Bazaar (2018), Lovin Dubai (2020) — Grasman, professionally known as Afra Drift, occupied a unique vantage point as a pioneer for women in Middle Eastern motorsport during the pivotal 2017 Saudi Royal Decree and the 2018 lifting of the driving ban.

This period provided critical data on human navigation during structural and social collapse. The concept of "Internal Continuity" was validated through Grasman's survival and recovery from a high-impact 2017 vehicle accident — the catalyst for formalizing the difference between optimization (machine logic) and survivability (human embodied logic).

// epistemic_note

Tafheena was not derived from existing frameworks but retrospectively positioned in relation to them. The observational corpus is single-participant and may reflect individual training history. The work presents a provisional hypothesis-generating model, not a validated mechanism. Empirical validation across independent participants and domains is the explicit goal.

GRASCODE: An Operational Architecture for Structured Decision Sequencing Following Boundary-State Control

GRASCODE is a structured decision-architecture framework designed to explain and support human action in environments where consequences are irreversible and time is limited. Traditional models of decision-making often assume stable conditions and sufficient time for analytical evaluation. However, real-world high-stakes environments — such as aviation incidents, emergency response, extreme operations, and survival situations — frequently disrupt deliberate reasoning and force rapid cognitive transitions.

The architecture identifies key phases including pattern recognition, mechanism identification, choice formation, idling stabilization, activation threshold crossing, responsibility commitment, decision execution, and transmission of consequences. Together these stages form a continuous control sequence that maintains functional stability when time pressure, uncertainty, and irreversible outcomes challenge conventional decision processes.
